## Leadership and Corporate Governance

**Date: 16 July 2026**

Following the official launch of SpamEDGE Company on **13 July 2026**, the company held its first Board Meeting of Shareholders on **16 July 2026** to establish its initial leadership structure and governance framework.

At the meeting, a formal vote was conducted in accordance with the company's governance principles. **Mr. Amusa Abdullahi Akintayo**, one of the founding members of SpamEDGE Company, was nominated for the position of **Managing Director**.

Although Mr. Amusa Abdullahi Akintayo is a founder of the company, SpamEDGE Company operates under a structured decision-making system in which key corporate appointments are determined through the appropriate voting process.

Following the vote, **Mr. Amusa Abdullahi Akintayo was duly elected and appointed as the Managing Director of SpamEDGE Company**, assuming responsibility for providing strategic leadership, overseeing the company's operations, and driving the organization's vision and objectives.

His appointment represents an important milestone in the company's development as SpamEDGE Company begins its journey toward establishing a strong corporate structure and expanding its operations across technology, engineering, construction, digital finance, philanthropy, and other strategic sectors.

The company remains committed to **good corporate governance, collective decision-making, transparency, professionalism, and accountability** as it continues to build a sustainable organization with a global outlook.

### A Leadership Built on Trust

With its leadership structure established through the collective decision of its shareholders, SpamEDGE Company begins its next chapter with a clear commitment to innovation, excellence, and sustainable growth.

Under the leadership of its newly appointed Managing Director, **Mr. Amusa Abdullahi Akintayo**, the company looks forward to transforming its vision into meaningful impact across Nigeria and the global community.
