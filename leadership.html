Founder & Managing Director

Amusa Abdullahi Akintayo
Founder & MD, SpamEDGE Company

Amusa Abdullahi Akintayo is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of SpamEDGE Company, a multidisciplinary company focused on delivering innovative solutions across Geospatial, Engineering, Construction, Architecture, and Technology.

He is a graduate of the Federal University of Technology, Minna, where he studied Surveying and Geoinformatics and obtained a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) degree in Surveying and Geoinformatics.

He has developed professional experience in the field of surveying and geospatial practice through several years of practical involvement in surveying projects. He worked as a Student Surveyor at A & T Surveying Technician for six years, gaining hands-on experience in surveying operations, field data acquisition, measurement, mapping, and other aspects of geospatial practice. He also undertook his Industrial Training (IT) at Seideq Geospatial Technology Limited, Lagos, where he further developed his practical knowledge and exposure to professional geospatial operations.

His areas of interest and expertise include Geospatial Technology, Surveying and Geoinformatics, Engineering, Digital Mapping, and Technology. Through his academic background and practical experience, he has participated in and contributed to a series of projects involving the application of geospatial knowledge and technology to real-world challenges.

As the Founder and CEO of SpamEDGE Company, Amusa provides strategic direction for the organization and is committed to building solutions that make professional geospatial services simpler, more accessible, efficient, and technology-driven. His vision is centered on using innovation and technology to make things easier for the geospatial world, while creating practical solutions that connect geospatial information with engineering, infrastructure, and emerging technologies.
