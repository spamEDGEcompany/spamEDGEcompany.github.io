About SpamEDGE

SpamEDGE Company is a technology-driven multidisciplinary company focused on Geospatial Intelligence, Architectural and Structural Technology, Engineering, Construction, and Sustainable Development.

Founded with a strong foundation in Surveying and Geoinformatics, SpamEDGE brings together spatial data, modern technology, design, and engineering to provide smarter solutions for the built environment and beyond.

Our approach connects geospatial data, architectural and structural design, technology, and infrastructure to help clients understand, plan, develop, and manage the spaces and assets around them.

The Expertise Behind SpamEDGE

SpamEDGE is founded on a professional background in Surveying and Geoinformatics, bringing expertise in spatial data, positioning, mapping, GIS, surveying technology, and the built environment.

Founder background: B.Tech in Surveying and Geoinformatics.

This geospatial foundation gives SpamEDGE a unique perspective: we understand the space, capture the data, analyze it, and transform that insight into practical solutions.
